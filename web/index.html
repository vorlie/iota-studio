GLSL Palette Manager

Paste const vec3 declarations, tweak the colors, copy the GLSL back out.

Multi-line declarations and negative values are fine.
Each color has its own space (OKLab, linear RGB or sRGB). OKLab colors are previewed the way your shader converts them (clamped to the sRGB gamut) and flagged if they fall outside it. Changing a color's space reinterprets its numbers, which is handy if you loaded it as the wrong one. The swatch picker converts back into the color's own space.
